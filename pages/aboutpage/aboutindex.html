Advancing Tunneling Technology

Dig 'Em Aggies is a student organization at Texas A&M University dedicated to advancing tunneling technology through teamwork, research, and engineering innovation. Our mission is to design and build a cutting-edge tunnel boring machine (TBM) that showcases the ingenuity and skills of our members while promoting sustainable and efficient underground construction practices.

Core Values

Community

Dig ‘Em Aggies exists to foster community among members and establish friendships and connections to last a lifetime.

Innovation

Dig ‘Em Aggies seeks to create innovation in the tunnel boring industry to meet local, regional, and national needs and advance the industry.

Sustainability

Dig ‘Em Aggies is focused on minimizing geological impact and protecting ecosystems by rejecting unnatural additives and being 100% electrically powered.

Mentorship

Dig ‘Em Aggies emphasizes inclusion of younger students to allow opportunities for leadership, teaching, and peer learning.

Development

Dig ‘Em Aggies enhances the education of Aggie engineers and prepares them for local industry opportunities in Bastrop, TX, and beyond.

Respect

Dig ‘Em Aggies is built upon respect for each other, our university, our sponsors, and the planet we seek to improve.

Faculty Advisor

Navid H. Jafari

Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering

Navid H. Jafari is a current and beloved professor at Texas A&M University. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in geotechnical engineering. He was awarded the prestigious ASCE Arthur Casagrande Professional Development Award in 2024, and his work in terrestrial, coastal, and offshore geotechnics has proven him as an emerging leader in the field.
