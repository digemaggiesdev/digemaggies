Our Mission

Drawing on the diverse talents of students across the Texas A&M Engineering Colleges, we aim to contribute innovative ideas to the future of tunneling infrastructure, while representing the Aggie spirit of leadership, integrity, and excellence.

The Vision

We seek to design and manufacture a horizontal boring machine, capable of boring and reinforcing a thirty meter tunnel. This will be much more complex, and we hope that our experience this year will ready us.

What is the Not-a-Boring Competition?

The Not-a-Boring Competition is an international engineering design competition with the goal of bringing innovation to the Tunnel Boring Industry. This competition consists of multiple design phases that conclude in a week-long dig, where teams from across the world come to Bastrop, Texas to showcase their innovations in tunnel boring machine technology.

Dig 'Em Aggies FAQ

What is Dig 'Em Aggies?

Dig 'Em Aggies is a student-led engineering team at Texas A&M University focused on tunnel boring machine design, construction, and innovation.

What is the Not-a-Boring Competition?

It is an international engineering design competition where teams develop tunnel boring technology and participate in a week-long dig in Bastrop, Texas.
